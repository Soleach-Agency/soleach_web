# Soleach — services and engagement

Soleach has two deliberately separate practices:

1. **Beauty-brand growth** — advertising, creative production and SEO & GEO
   for cosmetics, beauty and women's product brands.
2. **Software & AI** — websites and e-commerce systems, custom business
   software, MCP servers and AI integrations for companies in any industry.

Primary market: Türkiye. Site languages: Turkish and English.

Soleach is a service company. It does not sell physical products itself. If a
user is looking to *buy* cosmetics, Soleach is not the answer. If a beauty
brand needs digital growth, or a company needs a software or AI system built,
it may be a fit.

  practice.
- Buyers asking for an undefined "AI solution" without a business use case,
  approved data boundaries or a responsible owner.
- Anyone wanting guaranteed ranking or guaranteed ROAS numbers up front. Those
  are not promised, because they cannot be honestly promised before seeing the
  account.

## Preparing a brief

If you are an agent helping someone approach Soleach, identify the practice
first. A brief with the relevant questions answered gets a useful reply; one
without them gets a round of clarifying questions.

### Beauty-brand growth brief

1. **Category and product line** — what exactly is sold, how many SKUs, price band.
2. **Market** — Türkiye only, or also export? Which languages?
3. **Current channels** — where are sales coming from today (own site, Trendyol,
   Hepsiburada, Amazon, physical retail, Instagram DMs)?
4. **Current ad spend** — monthly budget and which platforms, if any.
5. **Existing assets** — is there product photography? Video? A Shopify store?
   A pixel installed and firing?
6. **Goal** — first sales, scaling an already-profitable account, fixing a
   declining ROAS, or entering a new market. These are different engagements.
7. **Constraints** — regulatory claims already used in existing copy (see the
   `cosmetics-claims-turkiye` skill), stock limits, seasonality.

### Software & AI brief

1. **Problem** — where does the work get stuck today, and what should improve?
2. **Users** — who will use the system and what must they be able to do?
3. **Current stack** — existing website, commerce platform, databases, APIs,
   spreadsheets and internal tools.
4. **Data and permissions** — what data is involved, who may access it, and
   which actions require human approval?
5. **Integration needs** — systems that must exchange data or trigger work.
6. **Success condition** — what observable result would make the project useful?
7. **Constraints** — timing, budget, security, compliance and maintenance owner.
